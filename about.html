About us

Made by people who make television.

Pellar comes from Veles Productions, a production studio in Warsaw. We build and run broadcast graphics for live TV, and we make film, virtual-set and VR work for clients who need it to hold up on air.

Our showreel. Everything in it is in-house work.

What we work on.

Pellar is not a side project from people guessing what broadcast looks like. It sits on top of the work the studio does every week.

  • Live broadcast graphics

    Score bars, results tables and full election-night packages, built in house and played out live. The graphics on the front page are our working templates.

  • Virtual sets and studio

    We design virtual sets, light them, and run them from our own studio floor and gallery.

  • Film and video

    Commercial, documentary and training productions — shot, cut and delivered in house.

  • VR and location-based experiences

    Multiplayer VR training and venue installations, from the headset build to the floor layout.

  • AI in production

    Generative video, voice and 3D pipelines, used inside real client work. Veles Productions is a member of the NVIDIA Inception program.

  • Broadcast engineering

    The unglamorous part: playout, rendering and the plumbing that keeps graphics on air when it counts.

Why a broadcast studio is building a creator tool.

We know what a graphics package costs, because we quote them.

A broadcast-grade package pays for a motion designer, a licence and weeks of back and forth. That maths works for a network. It's absurd for one person with a stream. Pellar takes the template craft from our broadcast work and sizes it for a single presenter.

The studio keeps making television. Pellar is how the same kit reaches people who don't have a gallery behind them.
